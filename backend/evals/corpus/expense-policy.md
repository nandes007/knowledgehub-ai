# Expense Policy

All expenses are submitted in Expensely within 30 days of the spend date.
Claims older than 60 days are rejected automatically.

## Approval thresholds

| Amount (IDR) | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 1,000,000 | No pre-approval, manager reviews the claim |
| 1,000,001 – 5,000,000 | Line manager, before the spend |
| 5,000,001 – 25,000,000 | Department director, before the spend |
| Above 25,000,000 | CFO, before the spend |

## Domestic travel caps

| Category | Cap per day (IDR) |
| --- | --- |
| Hotel | 1,200,000 |
| Meals | 400,000 |
| Local transport | 250,000 |

Meals are capped at IDR 400,000 per day in total, not per meal. Alcohol is
never reimbursable, including on client dinners.

## International travel caps

| Region | Hotel cap per night (IDR) | Meals per day (IDR) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Southeast Asia | 2,000,000 | 700,000 |
| East Asia and Australia | 2,800,000 | 900,000 |
| Europe and North America | 3,500,000 | 1,100,000 |

Economy class is the default for all flights. Flights with a scheduled
duration above eight hours may be booked in premium economy with director
approval. Business class requires CFO approval.

## Client entertainment

Client meals are reimbursable up to IDR 750,000 per attendee. You must record
the client company name and every attendee in the claim; claims without
attendee names are rejected.

## Non-reimbursable

The following are never reimbursable: traffic and parking fines, personal
subscriptions, in-flight wifi on flights under three hours, hotel minibar,
gifts to government officials of any value, and alcohol.

## Reimbursement timing

Approved claims are paid with the next payroll run if approved before the 20th
of the month. Claims approved after the 20th are paid the following month.
