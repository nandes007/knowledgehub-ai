# Employee Handbook

The handbook applies to every Nandes Tech employee, including contractors on a
retainer of six months or longer. It is reviewed every January.

## Working hours

Standard working hours are 09:00–18:00 Jakarta time (WIB), with a one-hour
lunch break taken between 12:00 and 14:00. Core collaboration hours, when
everyone must be reachable, are 10:00–16:00 WIB.

Employees may shift their start time by up to two hours with their manager's
written approval, as long as core hours are still covered.

If a holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday it is not moved to a weekday.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid annual leave per year, accruing at
1.67 days per completed month. Leave must be requested in Bamboo at least five
working days in advance; requests longer than ten consecutive days need
director approval.

Up to five unused days may be carried into the following year and must be used
before 31 March, after which they expire.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from annual leave and capped at 14 paid days per year. A
doctor's note is required from the third consecutive day of absence. Notify
your manager before 10:00 WIB on the first day of absence.

## Probation

New employees serve a three-month probation period. During probation, either
side may terminate the contract with two weeks' notice. After probation the
notice period is one month for individual contributors and two months for
managers and above.

## Performance reviews

Reviews run twice a year, in June and December. Each cycle combines a
self-assessment, manager review, and two peer reviews. Promotion decisions are
made only in the December cycle.
