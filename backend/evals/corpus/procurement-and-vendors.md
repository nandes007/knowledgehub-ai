# Procurement and Vendors

How Nandes Tech buys software and services. Expense reimbursement for
individual spend is covered by the expense policy; this document covers
contracts and recurring vendor relationships.

## When procurement applies

Procurement applies to any recurring contract, any commitment above IDR
25,000,000 per year, and any tool that processes customer data regardless of
price. One-off purchases below IDR 25,000,000 that touch no customer data go
through the normal expense process instead.

## Steps

1. Raise a request in the procurement tracker with the business case and the
   expected annual cost.
2. Security review, if the tool touches customer data. Allow ten working days.
3. Legal review of the contract and data processing agreement.
4. Finance approval against the department budget.
5. Signature. Only the CEO and CFO may sign contracts on behalf of the company.

## Preferred vendors

| Category | Preferred vendor |
| --- | --- |
| Cloud | AWS |
| Observability | Grafana Cloud |
| Identity | Okta |
| Password management | 1Password |
| HR and leave | Bamboo |
| Expenses | Expensely |

Using a non-preferred vendor in these categories requires director approval and
a written justification.

## Renewals

Vendor owners are notified 90 days before renewal. Any contract renewing above
IDR 100,000,000 per year requires a fresh market comparison of at least two
alternatives.

## Payment terms

Standard payment terms are net 30. Prepayment for more than one year is not
permitted without CFO approval.

## Vendor offboarding

When a contract ends, the vendor owner confirms data deletion in writing and
files the confirmation with Legal within 30 days.
