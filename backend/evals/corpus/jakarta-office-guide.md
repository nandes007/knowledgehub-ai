# Jakarta Office Guide

Practical information about the Jakarta office. Policy questions about how
often you must be in the office are answered by the remote work policy.

## Location and access

The office is on floors 11 and 12 of Menara Sentra, Jl. Jenderal Sudirman.
Access is by badge; the building is open 06:00–22:00 on weekdays and
08:00–18:00 on Saturdays. Weekend access outside those windows needs a request
to Facilities one working day in advance.

## Desk booking

Desks are booked in Deskly up to two weeks ahead. Bookings not checked in by
10:30 are released to the pool. Floor 12 is a quiet floor: no calls, no
meetings.

## Meeting rooms

| Room | Capacity | Equipment |

| Krakatau | 12 | Screen, camera, whiteboard |

| Semeru | 4 | Whiteboard only |

Rooms are released automatically if nobody checks in within ten minutes.

## Parking

The building has 40 reserved parking bays, allocated by ballot each quarter.
Motorcycle parking is unlimited and free. Parking fines are not reimbursable.

## Visitors

Register visitors in Deskly before 16:00 the previous day. Visitors must be
met at reception and accompanied at all times. Visitors do not get wifi on the
staff network; use the guest network.

## Facilities

Free coffee, tea, and fruit are provided in the floor 11 pantry. Lockers are
available on request. The prayer room is on floor 11, and the nursing room is
on floor 12 and bookable in Deskly.
