# Expense Policy 2024 (Archived)

Superseded by the current expense policy. Retained for audit of claims filed
before 1 January 2025. The caps below no longer apply.

## Approval thresholds (2024)

| Amount (IDR) | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 500,000 | No pre-approval |
| 500,001 – 3,000,000 | Line manager |
| 3,000,001 – 15,000,000 | Department director |
| Above 15,000,000 | CFO |

## Domestic travel caps (2024)

| Category | Cap per day (IDR) |
| --- | --- |
| Hotel | 900,000 |
| Meals | 300,000 |
| Local transport | 180,000 |

## International travel caps (2024)

| Region | Hotel cap per night (IDR) | Meals per day (IDR) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Southeast Asia | 1,500,000 | 500,000 |
| East Asia and Australia | 2,100,000 | 650,000 |
| Europe and North America | 2,600,000 | 800,000 |

## Client entertainment (2024)

Client meals were reimbursable up to IDR 500,000 per attendee. Alcohol was
reimbursable up to IDR 200,000 per attendee on client dinners; this exception
was removed in the current policy.

## Submission window (2024)

Claims were submitted within 45 days of the spend date and rejected after 90
days.
