# Benefits and Insurance

Benefits start on your first day; there is no waiting period. Coverage for
dependants must be requested within 30 days of joining or of a life event such
as marriage or a birth.

## Health insurance

Private health insurance is provided through SehatPlus and covers inpatient and
outpatient care.

| Cover | Annual limit per person (IDR) |
| --- | --- |
| Inpatient | 250,000,000 |
| Outpatient | 15,000,000 |
| Dental | 6,000,000 |
| Optical | 3,000,000 |

Dependants are covered for a spouse and up to three children. Parents are not
covered.

## Wellness allowance

Every employee receives IDR 3,600,000 per year for wellness. It covers gym
memberships, sports equipment, therapy, and meditation apps. It does not cover
supplements or cosmetic treatments. Claims go through the normal expense
process and expire at year end.

## Learning budget

The learning budget is IDR 15,000,000 per employee per year for courses,
conferences, and books. Conference travel is charged to the learning budget,
not the travel budget, and follows the travel caps in the expense policy.
Unused learning budget does not carry over.

## Parental leave

Birthing parents receive 16 weeks of fully paid leave. Non-birthing parents
receive 8 weeks, which may be taken in up to three blocks within the first year
after birth or adoption. Adoption is treated the same as birth.

## Retirement

Nandes Tech contributes to BPJS Ketenagakerjaan at the statutory rate and adds
a 3% voluntary employer contribution to a private pension for employees past
probation.

## Mental health support

Six counselling sessions per year are available through the SehatPlus employee
assistance programme at no cost, booked directly and confidentially. These
sessions do not consume the wellness allowance.
