# Remote Work Policy

Nandes Tech is hybrid by default. This policy covers where you may work, what
equipment we provide, and what is expected of you when you are not in the
Jakarta office.

## Office attendance

Employees are expected in the Jakarta office two days per week. Teams pick
their own anchor days and publish them in the team charter; most engineering
teams use Tuesday and Thursday. Fully remote arrangements require a written
exception approved by a director.

## Working from abroad

You may work from outside Indonesia for up to 30 calendar days per year without
a contract change. Stays longer than 30 days create tax and permanent
establishment risk and must be approved by People Ops and Finance before you
book travel. Time zones matter: you must still cover the 10:00–16:00 WIB core
hours defined in the employee handbook, or agree an alternative overlap with
your manager in writing.

## Home office stipend

Every employee receives a one-time home office stipend of IDR 7,500,000 on
joining, plus IDR 2,500,000 per year for renewals. The stipend covers desks,
chairs, monitors, and headsets. It does not cover rent, electricity, or
internet subscriptions.

Purchases above IDR 3,000,000 in a single item need manager pre-approval and
are claimed through the normal expense process.

## Internet allowance

Remote employees receive IDR 500,000 per month as an internet allowance, paid
with salary. No receipts are required.

## Working from public spaces

Cafés, coworking spaces, and airport lounges are permitted for regular work,
but never for handling customer data. Before working from a public network you
must connect to the company VPN and enable the privacy screen on your laptop,
as required by the IT security policy. Video calls that discuss customers,
salaries, or incidents must not be taken in public spaces.

## Equipment return

Company hardware must be returned within five working days of your last day.
Unreturned equipment is deducted from the final salary payment at book value.
