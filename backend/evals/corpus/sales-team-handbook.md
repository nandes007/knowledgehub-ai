# Sales Team Handbook

Rules specific to the sales organisation. Everything not covered here follows
the company employee handbook, expense policy, and travel caps.

## Quota and commission

| Segment | Annual quota (IDR) | Commission rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SMB | 4,000,000,000 | 8% |
| Mid-market | 9,000,000,000 | 6% |
| Enterprise | 18,000,000,000 | 5% |

Commission is paid quarterly, one month after quarter close, and only on cash
collected. Deals clawed back within 90 days reverse the commission.

## Accelerators

Attainment above 100% of quota pays 1.5x the base rate for the portion above
target. Above 130% attainment pays 2x. Accelerators reset each fiscal year.

## Discount approval

| Discount | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 10% | Account executive |
| 11–20% | Sales director |
| 21–30% | VP Sales |
| Above 30% | CEO and CFO |

Multi-year prepaid deals may add a further 5% without extra approval.

## Customer travel

Client visits are booked through the normal travel process and follow the
company expense caps; sales has no separate travel budget. Client
entertainment above the standard per-attendee limit needs VP Sales approval in
advance.

## CRM hygiene

Every opportunity must have a next step and a close date. Opportunities
untouched for 30 days are moved to nurture automatically. Forecast categories
are updated every Monday before 12:00 WIB.

## Sales onboarding

New account executives complete a two-week ramp: product certification in week
one, and three shadowed customer calls plus a mock pitch in week two.
