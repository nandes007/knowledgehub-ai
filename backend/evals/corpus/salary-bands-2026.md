# Salary Bands 2026

Bands are gross monthly base salary in IDR, excluding bonus and equity. They
are reviewed every January against a market survey and apply to the Jakarta
location.

## Bonus

The annual company bonus target is one month of base salary at 100% company
performance, paid in March. Individual multipliers range from 0.5 to 1.5 based
on the December review rating.

## Equity

Equity is granted at E3 and above, and at P3 and above. Grants vest over four
years with a one-year cliff, vesting monthly thereafter.

## Salary increases

Base salary changes happen once a year, effective 1 April, following the
December review cycle. Out-of-cycle increases are limited to promotions and
counter-offers and need CFO approval.

## Confidentiality

Band ranges are shared company-wide. Individual salaries are confidential and
visible only to the employee, their management chain, People Ops, and Finance.
